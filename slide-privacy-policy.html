Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SLIDE ("SLIDE", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the SLIDE mobile application (the "App").

SLIDE is a puzzle game designed to be playable primarily offline. We aim to collect and use only the information reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, improve, monetize, and support the App.

By installing or using SLIDE, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

SLIDE does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or other directly identifying information in order to play the game.

2.1 Information stored on your device

The App may store gameplay and preference information locally on your device, including:

  • Level progress and completion status
  • Stars earned
  • Coins, gems, keys, and other in-game rewards
  • Unlocked and equipped cosmetic items
  • Daily Challenge progress and streaks
  • Achievements and gameplay statistics
  • Hint and gameplay statistics
  • Audio, haptic, and other settings
  • Other data required for the App to function correctly

This information is generally stored locally and is not transmitted to us simply because you play the game.

2.2 Advertising information

SLIDE may use Google AdMob to display advertisements and provide optional rewarded advertisements.

Advertising providers may automatically collect certain information from your device in accordance with their own privacy policies. Depending on applicable settings and laws, this may include information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device and app information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from network information
  • Advertising interaction information
  • Diagnostics and performance information

Google may use this information to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising services, subject to applicable settings and legal requirements.

For more information, please review Google's privacy documentation: Google Privacy Policy .

2.3 Purchases

SLIDE may offer optional in-app purchases, including virtual currency and an option to remove advertisements.

Purchases are processed through Google Play and may be managed with RevenueCat.

We do not directly receive or store your full payment card number, bank account information, or other sensitive payment credentials. Payment processing is handled by the relevant platform or payment provider.

RevenueCat may process information necessary to manage purchases, subscriptions or entitlements, depending on the products offered by the App.

For more information, please review: RevenueCat Privacy Policy .

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by SLIDE and its service providers may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and maintaining the App
  • Saving gameplay progress and preferences
  • Providing rewarded advertisements
  • Processing and restoring purchases
  • Managing in-app purchase entitlements
  • Preventing abuse or fraudulent transactions
  • Diagnosing technical problems
  • Improving App stability and performance
  • Understanding advertising performance
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

5. Children's Privacy

SLIDE is not specifically designed as a service for children under the age required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Advertising and other third-party services may apply their own age-related restrictions, safeguards, and data-processing practices.

6. Data Retention

Gameplay data stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted by the App, cleared through your device settings, or the App is uninstalled, subject to the behavior of the operating system and any applicable third-party services.

Information processed by third-party providers is retained according to their respective policies and applicable legal requirements.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled in connection with SLIDE.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information processed by third-party services.

8. Your Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information processed by third-party services.

You may also control certain advertising and privacy preferences through your Android device and Google account settings.

You can stop the collection of information by SLIDE by uninstalling the App. You may also clear locally stored App data through your device settings, although doing so may remove your local gameplay progress and settings.

9. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers used by SLIDE may process information on servers located in countries other than your own.

Where applicable, such processing is carried out subject to the safeguards and legal requirements imposed by the relevant privacy laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to SLIDE, its services, third-party providers, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

We encourage you to review this page periodically for the latest information.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of SLIDE, please contact us through the contact information provided on the official SLIDE website or Google Play listing.
