Macropad Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Macropad ("Macropad", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Macropad application and related services.

1. Information We May Collect

Macropad is designed to minimize the information required to operate the service. Depending on the features you use, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Purchase information: Information necessary to manage subscriptions, purchases, entitlements, and restore purchases.
  • Device information: Device type, operating-system version, application version, language, diagnostic information, and technical identifiers where required by our service providers.
  • Application configuration: Profiles, buttons, macros, and related settings that you create within Macropad.
  • Connection information: Information necessary to establish and maintain a connection between your mobile device and your paired computer.
  • Support information: Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

2. Keyboard Input

Macropad may provide a virtual keyboard that allows you to type into your paired computer.

Text entered through this feature is transmitted to your paired computer for the purpose of performing the requested input.

Macropad is not intended to operate as a background keylogger. We do not intentionally collect everything you type on your device merely because the virtual keyboard feature is available.

We also do not intentionally collect touchpad movements merely for the purpose of monitoring your behavior.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Macropad.
  • Maintain connections between devices.
  • Process purchases and subscriptions.
  • Provide premium entitlements.
  • Maintain and improve the application.
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • Protect the service against abuse and security threats.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Local Data

Macropad may store profiles, macros, and configuration data locally on your device.

Depending on the version of Macropad and the features you use, certain information may also be processed by our servers or third-party service providers.

We aim to collect only information reasonably necessary to provide and improve the service.

5. Third-Party Services

Macropad may use third-party providers for services such as:

  • App distribution
  • Purchases and subscriptions
  • Subscription management
  • Analytics
  • Crash reporting
  • Hosting
  • Authentication
  • Security and infrastructure

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions may be processed through platforms such as Google Play, Apple App Store, RevenueCat, or other authorized providers.

We may receive information necessary to determine whether a purchase or subscription is active and to provide the corresponding Macropad Pro entitlement.

Payment card and payment credentials are generally handled by the applicable payment provider rather than directly by Macropad.

7. Analytics and Diagnostics

We may use analytics and diagnostic tools to understand application performance, crashes, general feature usage, and technical issues.

We do not intend to use these tools to collect the contents of your private keyboard input or personal macro content unless a future feature specifically requires such processing and the applicable privacy notices are updated accordingly.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate Macropad, process purchases, provide infrastructure, maintain security, analyze application performance, or provide customer support.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our users, rights, property, or services.

9. Future Features

Macropad is an evolving product and our data practices may change as new features and services are introduced.

If we introduce functionality that materially changes how personal information is collected or used, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

We will not rely on this section as permission to secretly collect sensitive information. Material changes to our data practices will be reflected in the applicable privacy disclosures.

10. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing the service, maintaining records, resolving disputes, preventing abuse, and complying with legal obligations.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

However, no electronic system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

13. Children's Privacy

Macropad does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us in a manner that violates applicable requirements, please contact us.

14. International Data Processing

Some of our service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international data transfers.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Macropad develops and our services or data practices change.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated.
